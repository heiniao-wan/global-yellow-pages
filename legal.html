关于本站About This Site

「全球黄页」是一个纯静态的网站导航与口碑评价目录，收录全球各行业的知名站点，并参考主流流量分析平台通用的行业分类法进行组织。

本站提供三项功能：① 按行业分类浏览与关键词搜索；② 由第三方截图服务自动生成的站点首页缩略图；③ 用户投票（点赞 / 不喜欢 / 无法访问）与相应的本机统计图表。

本站不设用户账号、不要求注册。投票数据保存在站长的服务器上，全站共享同一份统计；每台设备对同一站点只保留一票，可随时取消或改票。本站与所收录的任何网站均无隶属、投资或合作关系，收录本身不构成推荐或担保。

"Global Yellow Pages" is a static website directory and community-review catalog covering well-known sites across industries worldwide, organized according to industry taxonomies commonly used by mainstream traffic-analytics platforms.

The site offers three functions: (1) category browsing and keyword search; (2) homepage thumbnails auto-generated by third-party screenshot services; (3) user votes (Like / Dislike / Unreachable) with site-wide shared statistics — vote counts live on the operator's server, so every visitor sees the same totals. Each device keeps a single vote per site and may undo or change it at any time.

We have no user accounts and require no registration; all voting data stays in the visitor's own browser. This site is not affiliated with, nor endorsed by, any listed website, and inclusion is not a recommendation or warranty.

隐私政策Privacy Policy

1. 我们收集的信息

本站不设账号系统、不要求注册、不收集姓名、手机号等直接身份信息。我们收集的信息分为三类：

（一）你在浏览器本地存储的信息

以下内容保存在你自己的浏览器（localStorage），我们无法读取，仅用于让功能在你下次打开时保持一致：

  • 界面偏好（明暗主题、界面语言）；
  • 「我的收藏」列表；
  • 投票记录缓存（加密保存，带完整性校验，可发现并拒绝被篡改的数据）。

（二）发送到本站服务器的信息

为了让投票在全站共享（所有人的票数汇成同一份统计），你的投票请求会包含以下内容：

  • 匿名设备指纹标识——由 Canvas / WebGL / 时区等浏览器特征在你的设备本地生成，用于识别同一设备的重复投票，防止恶意刷票。发送前它会先经服务器用一把只有站长掌握的密钥做不可逆的哈希运算，服务器与数据库里保存的都只是哈希值，不保存指纹原文，也没有任何办法由哈希值反推回你的设备；
  • 你投的选项（好用 / 一般 / 无法访问）与所投站点的标识；
  • 请求到达时的出口 IP 地址（用于限流与防止同一网络大量刷票）。

上述信息保存在站长的 Cloudflare KV 存储中。只有票数统计与「该设备对某站投了哪一项」会被保留，不保存任何可直接识别你个人的信息。

（三）你主动提交的信息

  • 如果你通过「网站反馈」表单提交推荐，你主动填写的联系方式（电话或邮箱）与反馈内容——这些信息将仅保存在站长的留言后台（加密存储于 Cloudflare，仅站长凭密钥可见），不会发送到任何邮箱、不会公开显示，仅用于核实与回复；

2. Cookie 与第三方服务

本站自身不设置跟踪 Cookie。页面会向以下第三方服务请求资源：首页截图服务（Microlink、WordPress mShots）与网站图标服务（Google Favicon）。这些第三方会按其各自的隐私政策接收你的 IP 地址，并可能设置自己的 Cookie。建议阅读它们的官方政策。

我们处理上述数据的法律依据是提供核心功能（防滥用投票、实现全站共享统计）所必需的正当利益，并对哈希后的设备标识与投票记录施加了最小化、限期的处理。

1. Information We Collect

This site has no accounts, no sign-up, and collects no direct identifiers such as your name or phone number. Information we handle falls into three groups.

(a) Kept in your browser only

Stored in your browser's local storage, unreadable by us, used only so features stay consistent between visits:

  • Interface preferences (theme, language);
  • Your "favorites" list;
  • A cached copy of your voting records (encrypted with an integrity check so tampered data is detected and rejected).

(b) Sent to our server

So that votes are shared across the whole site (everyone's counts aggregate into the same statistics), a vote request includes:

  • An anonymous device-fingerprint identifier — generated locally on your device from browser traits such as Canvas / WebGL / timezone, used to recognize duplicate votes from the same device and prevent vote manipulation. Before it is sent, the server applies a one-way hash with a key only the operator holds; both the server and the database store the hash only, so the original fingerprint is never stored and cannot be recovered from it;
  • The option you selected (useful / meh / unreachable) and the identifier of the site you voted on;
  • Your network's exit IP address at the time of the request (used for rate limiting and preventing mass voting from a single network).

These are stored in the operator's Cloudflare KV store. Only vote totals and the fact that a given device voted a given site a given way are retained — nothing that directly identifies you.

(c) What you submit yourself

  • If you submit a suggestion via the "Website Feedback" form, the contact details (phone or email) and content you provide — these are stored only in the operator’s private message console (encrypted on Cloudflare, visible only to the operator with a private key) and are never emailed anywhere and never displayed publicly, used solely for verification and reply;

2. Cookies & Third-Party Services

The site itself sets no tracking cookies. Pages request resources from third-party services: screenshot providers (Microlink, WordPress mShots) and the Google Favicon service. These third parties receive your IP address and may set their own cookies under their respective privacy policies.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense

We plan to serve Google AdSense ads. Once enabled: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites; Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads based on your visits. You may opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings or manage third-party cookies at aboutads.info.

4. Legal Basis & Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

The legal basis for processing the above is legitimate interests in providing core functionality — abuse-resistant voting and site-wide shared statistics — with the hashed device identifier and vote records kept minimised and time-bounded.

How to exercise your rights:

  • Withdraw / erase — you can cancel a vote at any time, or ask the operator to delete your server-side voting record via "Contact"; once deleted you may vote again;
  • Access & correction — to review or correct a message you submitted, contact the operator;
  • Clear local data — clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored locally.

Users in the EU/EEA and California retain statutory rights of access, correction, and deletion, exercised in these ways.

5. Children's Privacy

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

6. Data Retention

Local data is retained until you delete it. Server-side voting records are kept long term to form the shared site-wide statistics; cancelling a vote removes it from those totals. Submitted messages are kept until the operator has handled them or you ask for deletion.
