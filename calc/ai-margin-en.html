What this calculator deliberately leaves out

It shows the spread. It does not tell you what to do about it.

  • Four repairs ranked by dollars returned, not by margin points
  • What a usage cap actually saves once you count the revenue it removes
  • The overage price that beats the cap, and the margin it costs you
  • How to run the price change as a 6-week cohort test instead of a guess
How this works. The cost distribution is fitted to a Pareto curve from two observations you can estimate without exporting anything: the share of your bill carried by your top 10% of accounts, and the total bill. Everything is computed in your browser — nothing you type leaves the page. The server records an anonymous page view and nothing else: no cookies, no account, no ad script.
